The lake shaped like its name.

Oyster Lake

Lake 6 of 15, west to east · Dune Allen

Topsail Hill Preserve State Park. Photo by Alexander Hatley, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons.

About

Oyster Lake covers about 26 acres in Dune Allen. The name comes from the outline: a bent, shell-like basin that is easy to pick out among the smaller lakes on this part of the coast. The drawing on the map uses that bent outline as a reminder, not as a surveyed shore.

The lake can be seen from the road and from the Timpoochee Trail. Like Stallworth Lake nearby, it is a practical place for bird-watching along a developed stretch of 30A, where a dune lake still holds a recognizable form between houses, trail, and Gulf.

Public access

Look from the road and from the Timpoochee Trail. Bird-watching is the use described here. No formal launch is listed on this page.

Access rules, park fees, and reservation systems change. This page is a summary for orientation, not a permit or a promise that a shore is open.
