Campbell Lake

Lake 3 of 15, west to east · Topsail

Sea oats on the dunes at Topsail Hill Preserve State Park, which holds Campbell Lake’s watershed, lake, and outfall. The photograph is of the preserve, not a labeled view of the lake. Photo by Alexander Hatley, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons.

About

Campbell Lake covers about 110 acres at Topsail. What sets it apart is not size alone. The watershed, the lake, and the outfall to the Gulf all lie in protected state land. Few of the fifteen lakes can say that the whole run of the system, from upland drainage to the intermittent opening on the beach, is still inside a preserve.

That continuous protection is why this guide treats Campbell as perhaps the purest example of a Walton County coastal dune lake. People paddle, hike, fish, and watch wildlife on that public land. The lake is a full dune-lake system you can see without crossing a patchwork of private shores.

Public access

The basin and its surroundings are on protected state land at Topsail Hill Preserve State Park. Paddling, hiking, fishing, and wildlife watching are the uses described here. Stay on designated park access.

Access rules, park fees, and reservation systems change. This page is a summary for orientation, not a permit or a promise that a shore is open.
