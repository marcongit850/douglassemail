## Search

Commons and Openverse were searched for a freely licensed photograph of each of the 15 lakes. Files that name a different lake were rejected, including Alligator Lake in Columbia County and Redfish Lake in Idaho.

The only Commons photograph found that names Oyster Lake in Walton County is a close portrait of a green heron at the outflow (`File:Green heron at Oyster Lake Outflow.jpg`, The Bushranger, CC BY-SA 4.0). It does not show the lake, so it is not used as the page photograph. No freely licensed photograph turned up for Fuller, Morris, Campbell, Stallworth, Allen, Draper, Big Redfish, Little Redfish, Alligator, Eastern, or Camp Creek that the file text identifies as that lake.
